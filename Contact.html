About Me

Hello, my name is Thinh Do, and I am a passionate chess player and coffee enthusiast. I created this website to share my love for both coffee and chess with others. I hope you find the content informative and enjoyable!

The reason I like chess is that, when I was a child, I was fascinated by the strategy and thinking required to play the game. I also like coffee because it helps me stay focused and energized while playing chess. I believe that coffee and chess are the perfect combination for a great day of mental stimulation and relaxation.
