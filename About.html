About Coffee & Chess

A journey through history

♟️ History of Chess

Chess is one of the oldest strategy games in the world. It originated in India around the 6th century, where it was known as "Chaturanga". The game later spread to Persia, then to Europe, where it evolved into the modern chess we know today.

Over time, chess became a symbol of intelligence and strategy. Today, it is played worldwide, both casually and professionally, and is even considered a sport.

☕ History of Coffee

Coffee was first discovered in Ethiopia, according to legend, by a goat herder who noticed his goats became energetic after eating coffee berries. From there, coffee spread to the Middle East and became popular in places like Yemen.

By the 17th century, coffee reached Europe and quickly became one of the most popular drinks in the world. Today, coffee is a daily habit for millions of people.

🌍 A Cultural Impact

Both chess and coffee have influenced cultures around the world. Coffee houses were once places where people gathered to play chess, discuss ideas, and share knowledge.

Even today, the combination of coffee and chess represents focus, creativity, and connection between people.
